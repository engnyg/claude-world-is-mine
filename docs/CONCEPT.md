# ワールドイズマイン / WORLD IS MINE — concept

Fan-made lyric MV. Music / Lyrics: ryo (supercell) · Vocal: 初音ミク. Built with vocaloid-style-mv-pipeline
(NikusonP, MIT) + JIZURA 字面 (hakoniwa, MIT). No character likeness: the singer is never drawn; the film is told
with typography, chess pieces and props. All art is procedural (Canvas2D), no AI images.

The lyric text is not stored in this repository (it is not ours to publish): the film reads it at render time from
the local `analysis/lyrics_mv.lrc` (see README). This document refers to lines by what they do, not by quoting them.

## Thesis

**Her word is law, and the world physically obeys it.** Every demand she sings becomes a royal decree that the frame
carries out: articles are stamped, pieces line up, the camera bows. The one thing outside her rulebook (being held,
being protected) is what breaks the rules, and her own heart is the real danger.

## Logline

A self-crowned queen rules a strawberry-and-cream chessboard kingdom with a growing list of decrees for her silent,
slow king: notice my hair, look at my shoes, answer properly, bring the white horse, cake, pudding. When the king
finally moves, it is a sudden hug that pulls her out of a car's path, and her crown slips: the danger is her heart.

## World / metaphor

- **Chess.** She is the **Queen** (the strongest piece, goes anywhere), he is the **King** (moves one square at a time:
  quiet and unfriendly). The **white horse** is the white **Knight**. Kneeling = the pieces bow toward her.
- **The board is the world**: a pink / cream checkerboard floor in perspective, bending and spinning to her lines.
- **Sweets** are her rewards and bribes: shortcake, pudding, candy rain.
- **A road** at night for the climax: headlights, a yellow warning sign that ends up holding a heart.

## Shapes & motifs

- Master shape: **the crown zigzag** (her rule): wipes, dividers, HUD rules, slam frames, cream on the cake.
- Secondary: **the heart** (what she actually wants): iris transitions, beats, the warning sign at the end.
- Grid: **checker squares** (the board) as floor, transitions (checker flip) and title frames.
- State changes: the crown sits straight in verse 1, bounces in the choruses, tilts after the hug and on the end card.

## Typography voice

- Decrees: **Zen Old Mincho** heavy, ink on cream, vermilion hanko stamp.
- Pop outbursts: **Dela Gothic One** slams on sunbursts; asides: **M PLUS Rounded 1c**.
- HUD: "ROYAL DECREE 第NN条" counter in **DotGothic16**, heart-shaped beat ticks.
- JIZURA carries the running lyric: `candy` / `sakura` styles with dark-ink schemes by day, `gold` (ivory) at night.

## Signature moments

1. A dark stage, one spotlight; the crowned queen rises into it on the hook.
2. Title on the intro riff: the board slides up, pieces pop on the beats, a crown drops onto the logo.
3. The decree scroll: three numbered articles typed in, each with its icon (bow, shoe, speech bubble); a stamp of 可.
4. Spot the difference (the new hairstyle) with hand-drawn red circles.
5. The empty right hand: a glove in a spotlight, arrows blinking on the beat.
6. The stopwatch that gets crossed out; the sweets that rain on the beat.
7. The carousel white knight; the knight crossing the board while the court kneels.
8. The silent king: a slow push in grey-lilac light while the queen hops impatiently.
9. Tea time: shortcake and a wobbling pudding; a cloche comes down and the colour drains.
10. The held breath in the vocal gap (dark stage, spotlight), then the last chorus at sunset.
11. The night crossing: headlights, the king pulls the queen back, the car passes in a white flash.
12. The warning sign turns into a heart, the crown lands on it crooked. Dead stop → end card.
